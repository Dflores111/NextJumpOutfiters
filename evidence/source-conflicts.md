## Copy and factual limits

Use the supplied short public copy as the baseline. Layout edits may clarify headings, shorten repetition and reflect available assets; they must not expand product promises. Keep internal visual briefs, owner names, approval labels and integration implementation details out of normal customer-facing copy, except an essential limitation at the affected decision.

Do not claim increased payload/tow ratings, universal compatibility, certified installation, US manufacturing of every component, guaranteed turnaround, fleet capacity, inventory, measured conversion gains or customer ratings without the corresponding current evidence. Existing authorized-installer/dealer URL handles do not approve a badge or certification statement.

Contact details and hours are source-derived and must be reconfirmed before publication. Keep published business details consistent across body, footer and structured data once approved.
