pricing  /  Questions worth answering before you buy.
What is a replacement flatbed?  It replaces the original pickup bed with a platform that can be configured for the intended use. Storage, sides, racks, and camper equipment depend on the selected system. It is not the same thing as a bed rack placed inside the factory pickup bed.
How do I know which one fits?  Start with the vehicle make, model, year, and factory bed configuration. Other details may matter, including cab, wheelbase, fuel system, and existing modifications. A similar bed length is not confirmation of fit. Send the details rather than choosing a different truck in the builder.
What affects the price?  The platform, selected equipment, installation scope, and any vehicle-specific work affect the quote. Compare configurations on the same basis. An installed starting price and a hardware-only price describe different things. Your final quote should identify what is included and what remains separate.
Does a flatbed increase my truck’s payload rating?  A replacement bed does not by itself increase the truck’s rated payload, axle ratings, or gross vehicle weight rating. The completed configuration adds and removes weight. Review the finished vehicle and its load against the applicable vehicle limits with a qualified professional.
Can I use my existing camper?  Possibly, but the exact combination needs review. Share the camper make and model, truck details, and manufacturer’s mounting requirements. Similar dimensions or a brand logo on a website do not establish compatibility.
Do I have to choose every option now?  No. You can begin with a starting configuration and discuss future plans. Some upgrades need supporting components, so ask which later additions are practical for your specific build. Modular does not mean every option fits every setup.
What does the online estimate mean?  It is a planning estimate for the selected configuration. It is not an order or installation reservation. Review the listed installation basis and open items, then request a final quote for the actual vehicle and scope.
Can I buy the bed without installation?  Ask our team about the current purchase and fulfillment options for the configuration you want. Do not assume that a retail product’s shipping policy also applies to a complete flatbed. Confirm the arrangement before planning pickup, transport, or installation elsewhere.
How long will the installation take?  Timing depends on the agreed scope, required equipment, vehicle condition, and shop schedule. Confirm the planned dates with the team before making travel arrangements. Product availability and an available installation appointment are separate things.
What warranty applies?  Review the written terms for the goods and the installation, as well as any separate manufacturer coverage. Ask which coverage applies to your exact order and how a claim is handled. Do not assume a product warranty and an installation-workmanship warranty are the same.
