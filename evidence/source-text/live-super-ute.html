The Super Ute

The Super Ute is a collaboration build with Next Jump Outfitters, Adventure Built, Flated, and Trukd. See the transformation of Kelly Varney’s 2017 Ford F250 Lariat FX4 Crew Cab Short Bed into a Swiss Army Knife of Overlanding featuring Aluminum Flatbed and Tailgate by Next Jump Outfitters.

Next Jump Outfitters completely transformed my 2017 Ford Superduty into an absolute beast of a truck. NJO absolutely crushed the build and is the only shop in the entire NorthWest that I trust with my rigs.

The Tailgate

The Super Ute was our first build to use our new Tailgate option. Paired with our tool box set, it allows for use of a Flated Topper or and topper camper like a Go Fast or Project M.
